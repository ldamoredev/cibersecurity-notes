# Glossary

- **Asset:** anything whose confidentiality, integrity, availability, or
  accountable use matters.
- **Trust boundary:** a transition where an assumption about identity, input,
  authority, or execution changes.
- **Telemetry:** events and measurements a system emits; telemetry alone is not
  a detection.
- **Detection:** tested logic that turns observed behavior into an actionable
  signal with known false-positive and false-negative limits.
- **Residual risk:** risk that remains after controls and verification.
- **Authorization boundary:** the explicit limit of systems and data an
  operator may test.
